Questions

Before you book

What does the starting price of ₹999 actually include?

A one-on-one consultation with a Chartered Accountant to review your income and clear any doubts, plus preparation and filing of your income tax return for the year.

Who is this for?

Salaried individuals and professionals filing a standard personal income tax return who want to speak to a CA before they file, rather than fill out a form alone.

What if my case is more complex — business income, capital gains, multiple properties?

Tell us during booking. We'll let you know upfront if your return needs a different scope or fee before any work begins.

What happens if I miss July 31?

You can still file a belated return later, usually with a late fee and some restrictions on deductions. It's best to file on time — talk to us as soon as you can.

Is the consultation over call or in person?

Over a phone or video call, at a slot you choose. If you're in Calicut, an in-person meeting can be arranged on request.
